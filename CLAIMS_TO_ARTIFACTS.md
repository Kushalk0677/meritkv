# Claim-to-Artifact Map

The manuscript is the sole authority for claims. This map only links its
claims to repository evidence. Raw files retain stable engine IDs:
`shadow_kv_plus` is MeritKV, `shadow_kv` is MeritKV-Sem, and
`shadow_kv_plus_lite` is MeritKV-Lite. Historical reports and raw campaign
notes are provenance, not independent paper claims.

## Explicit Non-Claims

- Write-through vLLM, SGLang, and LMCache rows do not show production
  acceleration caused by MeritKV.
- Exact-prefix cache acceleration itself is not attributed to the admission
  controller; the controller's contribution is the reuse/admission decision.
- Semantic similarity alone is not a correctness guarantee for KV
  substitution.
- The custom HF splice is not a native-runtime cache correctness test.
- The Qwen float16 splice result is not attributed to architecture.
- Modelled energy estimates under `results/energy_estimates/` are not measured
  paper evidence.
- Historical reports, operator notes, and one-off extensions are retained for
  provenance and do not override the current paper or canonical aggregate
  summaries.
